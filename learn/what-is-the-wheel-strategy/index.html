What is a wheel strategy in options trading?

What the wheel actually is Two strategies, used together. 1. Sell a cash-secured put on a stock you would own. The stock holds, you keep the premium and sell another. The stock falls below your strike, you are assigned the shares - at a discount, because the premium came off your cost. 2. Now you own shares. So you sell covered calls against them. Every call you sell lowers your basis again. The shares get called away, you take the gain, and you start over. That is the loop. Puts until assigned, calls until called away.
