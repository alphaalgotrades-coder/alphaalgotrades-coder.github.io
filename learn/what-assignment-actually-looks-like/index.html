Assignment is not the loss. It is the next leg. You now own shares you already decided you would own — and shares can be worked.

Why the first loss did not decide the outcome

That opening put closed down $740, the worst day in the book's first week. Had the position been closed there, that is where the story ends. Instead the shares were taken, calls were sold against them, a second put was written below the market, and the cost basis was ground down week after week until the stock recovered into it.

The stock never returned to where the trade started. It did not have to.

This is not theory.
