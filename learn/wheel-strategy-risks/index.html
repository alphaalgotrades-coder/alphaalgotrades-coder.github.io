What are the main risks of using a wheel strategy?

The stock drops after you sell the put. Three choices. Do nothing. You hold shares above the market and wait. The basis never moves. Average down. Buy more shares cheaper. The basis falls, but you have added capital with nothing paying you for the risk. Sell into it. Calls against the shares, another put below the market. The basis falls AND you get paid to wait.
