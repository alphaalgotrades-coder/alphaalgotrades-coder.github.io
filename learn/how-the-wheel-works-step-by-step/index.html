How does a wheel strategy work step by step?

Before any trade: pick a stock you are willing to OWN. Selling a cash-secured put means one of two things happens. The stock holds, you keep the premium. The stock drops, you buy the shares at your strike. So the only question that matters up front: would I be fine owning this company at that price? If the answer is no, the premium does not matter. What the machine screens for: liquid options, premium worth the collateral, and names that are not one headline from zero. High premium is usually high premium for a reason. Paper account.
