Privacy

Last updated 21 September 2026

Short version: this site does not ask you for anything, does not set tracking cookies, and does not sell or share data. Here is exactly what happens when you use it.

This website

  • No accounts, no forms, no email sign-up. Nothing on alphaalgotrader.com asks for your name, email or payment details.
  • Visit counts. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It does not use cookies and does not identify you; we see totals, such as how many people opened a page, not who they were.
  • Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them, as it would on most websites.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service.

Links that take you elsewhere

"Start free" and similar buttons take you to our product page on Whop, where any sign-up or payment happens under Whop's own privacy policy. Links to YouTube, X, TikTok, Instagram and Telegram take you to those services, under their policies. Some of our links carry a tag such as utm_source=post so we can tell which post a visit came from. That tag describes the link, not you.

The AAT Live Desk and Google sign-in

Our own software signs in to our own YouTube channel through Google so it can open and schedule the Live Desk broadcast each day. It uses that access only to manage broadcasts on that channel. It does not read, collect or store anything about viewers, and the sign-in is stored only on our own machine. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • Use advertising trackers or retargeting pixels on this site.
